# Day 1 — First Session: Company Formation & EXIM Pre-requisites (19.07.21)

## Overview
This opening session of the export-import (EXIM) course welcomes participants from across India (each running businesses in garments, pharmaceuticals, spices, silk sarees, handicrafts, candles, agro-products, etc.) and lays out the course roadmap. The bulk of the session teaches the **foundational setup needed to legally start an export business in India** — choosing a company structure, registering it, obtaining the mandatory Import-Export Code (IEC) and Export Promotion Council membership (RCMC), opening a bank account, and building an online presence. A recurring theme is **value addition / product differentiation** — turning a commodity into a higher-margin product rather than competing on the raw item.

## Course Roadmap (what later sessions will cover)
- **Marketing & product selection** across countries; how to research and verify buyer sources.
- **Role of government in export promotion**, trade agreements, and various sourcing channels.
- **Incoterms**, export pricing, and how to quote.
- **Banking & international payments**, payment risk management.
- **Customs agents / CHA** and services to exporters.
- How to run an export business **with little or no investment**.

## Key Concept — Value Addition / Differentiation
- Don't just export a raw commodity — think of a **differentiated, value-added form** that earns more profit.
- Examples given: onion → **onion powder / onion rings**; turmeric and other spices → powders/blends; garlic; **hair packs**, **mushroom powder**, etc.
- Ask "what can make my product different from everyone else's?" — differentiation is what wins buyers and margin.

## Company Formation — Types of Business Structure
Choose the structure that fits your scale and credibility needs:

- **Sole Proprietorship** — simplest and fastest to register. Essentially needs only a **PAN card**; the trade name is used but the PAN's legal name governs. Easy documents to open a bank account; can even be registered at your **home address**.
- **Partnership** — requires a **Partnership Deed** (prepared via a Chartered Accountant) between the partners.
- **LLP (Limited Liability Partnership)**, **One Person Company (OPC)**, and **Private Limited Company** — registered under the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) / companies compliances. These carry **more credibility** with international buyers but involve more formalities.

### Naming the company
- The name is **very important**. Keep it reasonably short (avoid overly long names like "ABC Export Import Factory Private Limited").
- It can combine words but should relate to your **nature of business / activity**.
- **Check domain-name (.com) availability** for the chosen name.
- Note: for a proprietorship the **same name can exist in different cities** without issue; a **Private Limited** name must be **nationally unique** (registered centrally).
- You can register a **trademark** for the name/logo (not compulsory to start, but protects brand).

## Bank Account
- Open a **current/business account** at your nearest bank.
- Choice of **public-sector banks vs private banks** (trade-off between service and convenience).
- Typical documents: **PAN card, Aadhaar card, address proof** (plus the entity's registration/deed as applicable).

## IEC — Import Export Code (mandatory)
- **Required for anyone doing international trade** above the threshold; you cannot export/import commercially without it.
- Apply **online** at the DGFT portal: **dgft.gov.in**.
- Process: register on the site with **email + phone**, verify via **Aadhaar-based OTP**, upload required documents (PAN, bank details, address), and submit.
- The process is now **quick and online** (earlier it could take 2–3 months).
- **Annual updation** of the IEC is now required — update your details every year (typically April–June window) or the code can be deactivated.

## RCMC — Registration-Cum-Membership Certificate
- Issued by **Export Promotion Councils (EPCs)** under the Government of India, one per sector — e.g. **Gems & Jewellery, Electronics, Chemicals, Garments, Handloom, Pharma**, etc.
- Also relevant: **Commodity Boards** (Coconut Board, Tea Board, Spices Board, Tobacco Board, etc.) and **APEDA** (Agricultural & Processed Food Products Export Development Authority) for agri/processed-food exporters.
- Membership (free or paid depending on the council) gives access to **government export benefits/schemes** and **latest trade & industry information**.
- Apply online; RCMC membership certificate is used to claim government incentives.

## "Made in India" / Manufacturing
- Products should be **manufactured in India** and labelled accordingly to qualify as Indian-origin exports.

## Building Online Presence & Digital Marketing
- A professional **website** builds brand value and goodwill (the speaker cites how brand/website value can run into billions for big brands).
- **SEO**: rank on Google via good **keywords, content, product details, and images**; the website should surface on the first page for relevant searches.
- **Social media** — Facebook, Instagram, Twitter — plus **WhatsApp Business** with a downloadable **catalog**.
- Use **content writing** and even chatbots/auto-replies to engage visitors and capture leads (name, mobile, email) for follow-up.

## Samples & Courier
- Send **product samples** to serious buyers; some exporters send **free samples**, others recover **courier charges** (e.g. via Blue Dart), sharing the **courier account number / tracking number** with the buyer.
- Give samples selectively — typically only once a buyer shows genuine intent.

## Billing, GST & Buyer Database
- Prepare proper **invoices/bills** with company name, product, and **GST details**.
- **GST** is not needed merely to develop the business but is required for commercial billing.
- Maintain a **database of all buyers/leads** for follow-up; a Google form is used to collect participant questions and details.

## Required Documents / Registrations Checklist (as taught)
- **PAN card** (individual or entity)
- **Aadhaar card** (for OTP verification)
- **Address proof**
- **Partnership Deed** (if partnership) / MCA registration (if LLP/OPC/Pvt Ltd)
- **Bank current account**
- **IEC** (Import Export Code) via dgft.gov.in
- **RCMC** from the relevant Export Promotion Council / Commodity Board / APEDA
- **GST registration** (for commercial billing)
- Optional: **Trademark** registration for name/logo

## Portals / Bodies Named
- **DGFT (dgft.gov.in)** — issues IEC
- **MCA** — company registration (LLP/OPC/Pvt Ltd)
- **Export Promotion Councils (EPCs)** — issue RCMC by sector
- **Commodity Boards** (Coconut, Tea, Spices, Tobacco) and **APEDA** — agri/commodity exports
