# Day 1 — Second Session: Market Research on Export-Import Potential Products (19.07.21)

## Overview
This session teaches participants how to select a product to export and how to validate its potential using government trade-data tools. It walks through HS (Harmonised System) product codes, official data sources for export/import statistics, how to identify target countries and competitors, and quality/certification requirements — with a live Q&A addressing specific products (spices, jaggery, turmeric, organic food, Ayurvedic medicine, handicrafts, rice).

## Choosing Your First Product
- Selection factors: personal familiarity/expertise, existing manufacturing capability, whether the product needs **value addition**, and whether the product/category already has export demand.
- You do not need to be the manufacturer yourself — you can also work as a **trader/exporter** by sourcing from manufacturers, provided you have proper agreements and registration.
- Multiple products can be registered/exported under one IEC — you are not restricted to a single item.

## HS Code (Harmonised System) — Core to Market Research
- Every product has a unique classification code used in customs/trade data, organized into **Chapters** (2-digit), then further into 4, 6, and 8-digit sub-codes for increasing specificity.
- Examples discussed: turmeric, basmati rice, chilli powder, jaggery, spices — each falls under a specific chapter (e.g., agricultural products, spices chapters).
- Finding your HS code: search the product name online / verify via government HS code lookup; correct code is essential for accessing export data and any applicable duties/incentives.
- The last digits allow country-specific product description drill-down.

## Data Sources for Market Research (key portals mentioned)
- **commerce.gov.in** — Ministry of Commerce & Industry portal with district-wise export data and policy circulars.
- **Export-Import Data Bank** — under the Ministry of Commerce; shows net export/import figures by product/HS code and country.
- A dashboard/tool referred to for **Foreign Trade Policy** data.
- **Niryat Mitra** app — available on Play Store; helps identify export leads/data (mentioned as a free downloadable resource).
- Country trade portals such as **UN Comtrade**-type / international resources for cross-checking global import-export figures (referenced generally, e.g., checking which countries import a product and in what volume).

## What to Extract From the Data
For a chosen HS code / product, learn to find:
- **Which countries export this product**, and their **share (%) of world exports/trade**.
- **Which countries import this product**, and the trend (increasing/decreasing) over the last ~5 years.
- The **top exporting countries** (e.g., discussion of Italy, Tunisia as major olive oil exporters/producers) and how competitive your target market is.
- **State-wise / district-wise export data within India** (e.g., which Indian states/districts are already major exporters of a given product — examples cited: Gujarat, Maharashtra, Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, UP, Haryana for various products).
- Historical trend data (current year vs. previous years) to judge whether a product's exports are growing or shrinking.

## Government/Industry Bodies for Registration by Product Category
- **Export Promotion Council for Handicrafts (EPCH)** — for handicraft exporters; contact/registration details available on its website.
- Product-specific councils/boards (as in Session 1): e.g., **Spices Board**, **Coconut Board**, chemical/pharma-related councils — register with the board matching your specific product; if you deal in multiple products you may need to register with more than one board.
- **RCMC (Registration-Cum-Membership Certificate)** — confirmed again as required registration proof; discussed relative to which council to approach based on product category (e.g., a jaggery exporter vs. a handicraft exporter goes to different councils).

## Quality, Certification & Samples
- **Quality testing** is essential before export — buyers will reject product without it; testing should be done proactively, not only when demanded.
- **Organic certification**: required if marketing a product as "organic" (e.g., organic farming/jaggery) — a formal certification process exists; without it, you cannot legally claim "organic" status.
- **Ayurvedic/herbal medicine exports**: classified under a specific category; formulation and country-specific import regulations can be more complex (soil type, herb sourcing, and destination-country drug rules all matter).
- **Samples**: expect to send free or low-cost samples to serious buyers to demonstrate quality before larger orders are placed; courier costs are typically bilaterally agreed (buyer or seller pays).
- Manufacturing/registration license requirements are product-specific (e.g., a question on jaggery manufacturing and labelling licensing was raised — the manufacturer needs proper licensing and correct labeling for the product/quantity being sold).

## Q&A Highlights (illustrative real questions from participants)
- **Basmati rice to Kuwait**: participant noted demand already exists; advised to check current suppliers/competition data before entering, since an already well-supplied market may not be advisable without differentiation.
- **Turmeric export from Pune region**: discussed regional suitability and specific quality traits relevant to that turmeric variety.
- **Jaggery manufacturing/export from Gujarat**: covered manufacturer registration, labeling requirements, and limited-quantity local sales considerations.
- **Ayurvedic/herbal medicine exporter (Bengaluru)**: guided toward the appropriate product category and away from assuming a single "one-size" registration covers all herbal products.
- **Handicrafts/EPCH member (existing exporter)**: confirmed follow-up on registration/certificate matters through the council.

## Key Takeaways
- Product selection should be backed by **data, not guesswork** — always validate demand, competition, and trend direction using official government export-import data sources before committing.
- Correctly identifying your **HS code** unlocks access to all further research (data, duties, incentives).
- Match your **RCMC/registration** to the correct sector council — it varies by product and can require multiple registrations for multi-category businesses.
- **Quality assurance and (where relevant) certification** (e.g., organic) must be arranged in advance of dealing with international buyers.
- Use free government/industry tools (commerce.gov.in, Export-Import Data Bank, Niryat Mitra app) rather than relying only on informal market awareness.
